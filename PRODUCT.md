# Chapter

## Register
product

## Users
Developers with multiple personal projects who work at a desk in daylight, moving between focused work and planning. They need to find, share, pause, and resume projects without losing their context.

## Product Purpose
Keep every project ready for its next chapter. The core path is import, organize, explicitly share, park with memory, and return with a useful editable brief.

## Brand Personality
Calm, thoughtful, capable. Plain language and helpful, specific feedback.

## Design Principles
Prioritize the next action. Preserve context. Make privacy visible. Distinguish working integrations from unavailable services. Never fabricate project information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, visible focus, labeled inputs, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and status text alongside color.
